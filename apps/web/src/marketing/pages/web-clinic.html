The Commercial Arrangement

No setup fee until you approve the build for launch.

01

Discovery first

A short discovery session sets goals, design direction, pages, functionality, and the content you already have.

02

Written scope

Development starts only after the pages, features, and package are documented and agreed.

03

Reasonable revisions

We revise to match the agreed direction — not unlimited rebuilds until someone likes a sixth version.

Pay at launch approval

The setup fee is due when you approve the completed site for launch. It does not go live on your production domain until that fee is paid.
